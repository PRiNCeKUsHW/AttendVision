How it works

Three steps, no roll call, no paper register.

1

Students register with their face

A student opens the portal, looks at the webcam and enters their name. That snapshot becomes their FaceID. They can add a short voice sample too.

2

Teacher snaps the classroom

Create a subject, share its code or QR so students enroll, then add a few photos of the room from the camera or your gallery.

3

Review and save

AttendVision matches every face to an enrolled student. Check the list, fix anything it missed, and save the session with one tap.

Everything in one place
